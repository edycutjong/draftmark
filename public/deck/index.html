Three Meta models · three jobs

Each model does one load-bearing job.

sam-3.1 · video

Sees

Two video calls per clip: “white number” and “water”.

muse-spark-1.3

Reads

Two strict-schema label reads. Native computer-use filing on our demo portal: offline spike, read-back 6/6.

muse-wonder-1.0

Writes + proves

Writes the ship's survey program, regenerates on failing conditions: 3 calls, est $0.481.

Each model owns one stage: without SAM there is nothing to measure; without Spark the labels must be typed in (marked degraded); without Wonder there is no model-written program for a new booklet.
