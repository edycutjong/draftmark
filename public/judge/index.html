Line 003 · Limits

What this does not prove

Four of the six honest limits in the README, quoted (each cites its README number); all six are on the landing page.

  • 1
    “The weight check is on 12 loads of a rendered synthetic barge (code-drawn pixels, truth by physics), not a physical scale: it checks the chain's arithmetic, label checks and wave-averaging, not real water, glare or a real print.”README · Honest limits, 1 (abridged)
  • 2
    “The real-ship clips are third-party test footage with no ground truth (repeatability and refusal only, no accuracy claim); no real-ship draft accuracy is claimed.”README · Honest limits, 2
  • 3
    “The test booklet is a Ro-Ro/passenger ship, not a bulk carrier; a draft outside its 0.5-9.3 m table is refused, so the two real clips certify as REFUSE OUT_OF_TABLE.”README · Honest limits, 3 (abridged)
  • 4
    “This is a prototype, not a surveyor's document.”README · Honest limits, 6 (last sentence)
